Our Mission

Inuit economic sovereignty — built from the land up

North West Passage Holdings Corporation was founded on a simple belief: that Nunavummiut should own, lead, and benefit from the economic development happening in their homeland.

From Qikiqtarjuaq to the wider Qikiqtani region, NWPH is developing a portfolio of enterprises that create real, lasting employment and wealth for Inuit communities — without asking permission to participate in our own economy.

Founded by Adam Aliqatuqtuq, a Nunavut beneficiary with roots across the territory, NWPH draws on deep knowledge of Arctic operations, Inuit governance, and northern logistics to build something that will outlast any single generation.

Community-Rooted

Every subsidiary is designed to employ, empower, and enrich Nunavummiut — particularly youth aged 18 to 25.

Arctic-First

We build for northern realities — remote logistics, extreme climate, sovereignty, and the unique landscape of the Qikiqtani Region.

Diversified & Resilient

Seven subsidiaries spanning tourism, tech, retail, sealift, finance, aviation, and real estate — so Inuit prosperity doesn't depend on a single sector.

Inuit Leadership

Led by Nunavut beneficiaries, with co-founders, advisors, and staff drawn from across Nunavut's communities and professional networks.

Tourism & Expeditions

ArcTrek Expeditions

ArcTrek Expeditions Ltd.

Inuit-guided Arctic adventure tourism delivering authentic land-based experiences across Nunavut. Co-founded with Eva Natsiapik of Qikiqtarjuaq, ArcTrek connects visitors directly to Inuit knowledge, landscapes, and culture.

A northern financial services institution designed for Inuit and Nunavummiut — offering accessible banking, micro-lending, and financial literacy built around northern economic realities rather than southern banking models.

Youth & Community

Careers on the land, not just a job

The Nunami Ilinniarni program — "Learning on the Land" — is the soul of NWPH's community commitment. Every subsidiary is designed with a youth employment pathway built in, giving young Nunavummiut not just work experience, but ownership stakes in their own territory's economy.

From ArcTrek guides to Polar Beacons technicians, North Winds crew to NunaBank staff — we're building the next generation of Inuit economic leadership from the ground up.
