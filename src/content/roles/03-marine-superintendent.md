## The role

Accountable for the safe execution of the sealift season: vessel scheduling,
lightering operations, shore handling, and the safety systems that hold it all
together.

The season is short. A week lost in August is a community without building
material until next year, and this role exists to make sure that does not
happen.

## What you need

- Senior marine operations experience, ideally including Arctic or ice-affected
  waters
- Working knowledge of Transport Canada requirements for the operations we run
- Experience managing seasonal crews

## What we offer

- Permanent year-round position with seasonal intensity
- Northern living allowance and subsidised housing
- Relocation support to Iqaluit
