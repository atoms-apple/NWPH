## The role

Install, service and recover monitoring equipment in remote locations across the
Qikiqtani region, and keep it running between visits. Roughly a third of the
year is travel.

This is the company's fourth technical hire and its second this year.

## What you need

An electrical, instrumentation or IT background — or demonstrated aptitude and a
willingness to be trained. We recruit for judgement and reliability in the field
and teach the rest, because the entire point of this company is that the
capability lives here rather than flying in.

Two of our six staff joined on a structured training plan rather than with the
qualification in hand.
