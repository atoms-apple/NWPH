## The role

Assess and manage small business and equipment lending across the territory.

The underwriting here is not southern underwriting. Seasonal and harvesting
income is income; a thin credit file is the absence of local credit
infrastructure rather than evidence of risk. You will be expected to understand
that distinction and apply it.

## What you need

Credit, banking or co-operative lending experience. Inuktitut is a significant
asset in this role.
