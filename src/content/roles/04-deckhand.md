## The role

Shore and barge crew for the sealift season: cargo handling, lightering runs,
securing loads, and beach operations.

Physically demanding, weather-dependent, and time-critical. Crew are retained
year to year, and several of our current superintendents started as deckhands.

## What you need

Marine Emergency Duties certification, which we fund. Everything else is taught
on the job.
