## The role

Front of store, stock and receiving, in either community. Ongoing recruitment —
casual and permanent, full and part time.

Nine of our fourteen staff joined with no prior retail experience. If you have
not done this before, that is not a barrier.

## What you need

Nothing formal. Reliability, and a willingness to learn the stock system, which
is what keeps the live availability feed honest.

Applications are held for six months and considered as positions open in your
community.
