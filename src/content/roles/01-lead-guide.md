## The role

Lead multi-day land-based expeditions across the floe edge and open-water
seasons. You are responsible for the safety of the party, route decisions, camp
management, and the quality of what guests actually experience.

You will also mentor assistant guides working toward their own lead
certification, which is how this company replaces itself.

## What you need

- Extensive on-land and on-ice experience in the Qikiqtani region
- Wilderness first aid (80-hour) — we will fund this if you do not hold it
- Firearms safety, and demonstrated judgement around wildlife
- Small vessel operator proficiency, or willingness to obtain it

Formal qualifications matter less here than time on the land. If you have the
experience and not the tickets, apply anyway — funding certification is part of
the job offer, not a barrier to it.

## What we offer

- Seasonal contract with returning-guide priority each year
- Certification and re-certification paid in full
- Equipment provided
- Travel from your home community to the trip start
