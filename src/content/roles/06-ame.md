## The role

Scheduled and unscheduled maintenance on our fleet, working to the standards our
Air Operator Certificate requires. Cold-weather serviceability is most of the
job for half the year.

## What you need

- AME licence (M1 or M2), or apprentice working toward one
- Experience on turboprop types operating in remote conditions is an asset

## Apprenticeships

Two AME apprenticeship places are open each year to Nunavummiut, with
schooling costs and travel covered. Apply through the training pathway.
