## The role

Run the floor day to day: the team on shift, receiving, stock counts, and the
accuracy of what the app says is on the shelf.

That last part is not an afterthought. The company publishes live stock levels
publicly, and a count that is wrong is visible to the whole community within
minutes. Keeping it right is a core part of this job.

## What you need

Retail or supervisory experience is useful but not required. Knowledge of the
community matters more, and we will teach the systems.

## What we offer

- Staff housing
- Management training delivered in community, not down south
- A clear path to Store Manager; our Pangnirtung manager started on the floor
