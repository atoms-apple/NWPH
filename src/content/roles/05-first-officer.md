## The role

Line flying across the Qikiqtani region: scheduled service, charter, medevac
support and camp work, on gravel strips and short runways in weather that
changes fast.

## What you need

- Commercial Pilot Licence with Group 1 instrument rating
- Multi-engine experience; northern or gravel experience an asset
- Minimum hours per company policy

## Pathway

We sponsor Nunavummiut through flight training and type endorsement. If you are
working toward a licence rather than holding one, contact us about the training
pathway rather than applying here.
