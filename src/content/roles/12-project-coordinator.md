## The role

Coordinate delivery of residential builds from sealift planning through
handover: materials scheduling, trades coordination, and the apprenticeship
placements that run alongside each build.

## What you need

Construction coordination experience, and the organisational discipline that
building within a short season demands. Northern construction experience is an
asset but not required.
