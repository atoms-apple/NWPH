## The role

Plan and place the buy for both stores, and own the sealift consolidation that
carries most of it north.

Inventory here is bought and shipped a season before it sells. A buying error is
discovered eleven months later, by which point the only remedy is air freight at
several times the margin. This role exists to make fewer of them.

You would also own the data behind the public availability feed: reconciling
counts, investigating discrepancies, and deciding when a count is too uncertain
to publish.

## What you need

Inventory, purchasing or logistics experience. Comfort with spreadsheets and
stock systems. Northern supply chain experience is an asset but is more often
learned here than brought in.
