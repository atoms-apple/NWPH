Founder of North West Passage Holdings Corporation. Nunavut beneficiary and
entrepreneur from Qikiqtarjuaq, with a background spanning Parks Canada, the
North Warning System, Canadian North Airlines, the Qikiqtani Inuit Association,
and Government of Nunavut finance and administration.

Sits on the board as an executive director.
