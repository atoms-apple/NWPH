---
question: Does registering as a supplier commit NWPH to anything?
order: 1
draft: false
---
No. Registration records your details so that you can be contacted when a
relevant requirement is issued. It is not a contract, a pre-qualification, a
standing offer, or a place on an approved-vendor list — none of those exist yet.
It does not oblige NWPH to invite you to bid, and it does not oblige you to
respond.
