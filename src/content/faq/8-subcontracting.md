Yes, particularly in the sectors where NWPH does not expect to build a company
from nothing — aviation, marine freight and financial services are all more
plausible as partnerships than as start-ups.

Those conversations go through the [partnership enquiry](/contact/#partnership-form)
rather than supplier registration.
