No, and this is worth being direct about: NWPH is pre-incorporation. There are
no audited statements, no operating history and no credit file, because there is
no trading entity to have generated them.

Suppliers should weigh that when deciding whether to extend terms. When the
corporation has audited statements it will publish them.
