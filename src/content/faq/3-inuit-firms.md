---
question: Will Inuit firms receive preference?
order: 3
draft: false
---
NWPH intends to apply an Inuit firm and Nunavut-business preference in its
procurement, consistent with the obligations that apply to public-sector
contracting in the territory and with the corporation's own purpose.

The specific mechanism — how preference is weighted, what evidence is required,
and how it interacts with any funder's own rules — has not been set. It will be
published as policy before the first requirement is issued, not decided case by
case afterwards.
