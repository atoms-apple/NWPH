ArcTrek Expeditions has closed its twentieth season.

The company again ran fewer trips than demand would have supported. Capacity is
set by the number of experienced lead guides, and the company has consistently
chosen to run fewer trips rather than dilute them.

Four assistant guides advanced to guide during the season.
