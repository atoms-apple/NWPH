The board has resolved to wind up one of the corporation's operating
subsidiaries, which did not reach sustainable trading within the period set for
it at formation.

All affected staff have been offered positions in other operating companies.
Supplier obligations will be met in full.

"A development corporation that cannot close a subsidiary will end up carrying
one indefinitely, funded by the ones that work," the board said in its
resolution. "That is not a service to anybody."

This is reported here because a portfolio that only publishes its successes is
not reporting.
