Polar Beacons Technologies has renewed its multi-year environmental monitoring
agreements covering sites across the Qikiqtani region.

All service is delivered by technicians resident in Nunavut. The company holds
spares in the territory, so an equipment failure is a call-out rather than a wait
for a part to travel north.
