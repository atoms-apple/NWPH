The board has adopted a revised procurement policy across the holding company
and all operating subsidiaries.

The principal change is a published payment-terms commitment. Slow payment is a
serious problem for small northern suppliers, and a development corporation that
pays late has undermined its own purpose.

The policy is available on the [reports page](/reports/).
