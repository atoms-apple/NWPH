The 2026 sealift season has opened, with first landings scheduled through July
and August.

Ima Sealift Solutions will handle cargo into 13 communities this year. The
company is again running its returning-crew policy, under which seasonal crew
have first claim on their position from the previous season.

Community delivery schedules are issued directly to consignees.
