Four apprentices have been placed for the coming season: two on the guiding
pathway at ArcTrek and two in technical services at Polar Beacons.

Apprentices are employees from the first day rather than students on placement.
Schooling costs, travel and accommodation are covered in full.

Four is a small number. It is also four more than the territory had in these
roles at this corporation two years ago.
