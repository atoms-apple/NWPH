Polar Express Stores has opened its second location in Qikiqtarjuaq.

Five staff were recruited locally, bringing the company to fourteen across both
communities. Nine of the fourteen joined with no prior retail experience.

Buying for the new store is consolidated with Pangnirtung and planned onto
sealift a season ahead.
