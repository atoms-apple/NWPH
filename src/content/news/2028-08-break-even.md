Polar Express Stores has reached operating break-even, a year earlier than the
plan assumed.

The cause is not a sales surprise. It is that the Qikiqtarjuaq store shares
buying, warehousing and sealift consolidation with Pangnirtung, which took the
cost of the second store well below the cost of the first.

The company is not yet returning capital to the holding company, and was not
expected to at this stage.
