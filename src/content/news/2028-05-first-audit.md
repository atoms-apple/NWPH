The first annual general meeting was held in Iqaluit.

The board reported consolidated revenue of $2.4 million for the year ended
31 March 2028, covering the holding company and both operating subsidiaries, and
an unqualified audit opinion.

The corporation is not yet profitable at the consolidated level. The annual
report sets out where each company stands against its plan, including where it
does not meet it.
