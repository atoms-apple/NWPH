The annual general meeting was held in Iqaluit on 27 May.

The board reported consolidated revenue of $41.2 million for the year ended
31 March 2026, and an unqualified audit opinion. Full audited statements and the
annual report are available on the [reports page](/reports/).

Mary Major was re-appointed as Chair of the governance and nominating committee.
The board confirmed that three of its six directors remain independent of both
management and any funder.
