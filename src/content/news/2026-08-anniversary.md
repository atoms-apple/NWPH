North West Passage Holdings Corporation was incorporated in Nunavut in 2001 with
one subsidiary and a mandate to build companies in sectors where Nunavummiut had
no locally-owned alternative.

Twenty-five years on, the corporation operates seven companies employing 214
people across 13 communities, 68 per cent of them Nunavut Inuit.

"The measure was never how many companies we started," said Adam Aliqatuqtuq,
founder and Chair. "It was whether any of them could stand up without being
protected. Six of the seven can. We closed the one that could not."

The anniversary will be marked at the annual general meeting in Iqaluit.
