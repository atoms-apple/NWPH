ArcTrek Expeditions Ltd. has begun incorporation and territorial licensing, with
a first guided season targeted for summer 2029.

Guide recruitment has started and certification for the first intake is funded.
Financing is the step most likely to move the date, and the corporation would
rather say that now than explain a slip later.

ArcTrek is not taking bookings and will not until a season is confirmed.
