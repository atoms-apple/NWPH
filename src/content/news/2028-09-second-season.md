ArcTrek Expeditions has closed its second season.

The company again ran fewer trips than demand would have supported. Capacity is
set by the number of experienced lead guides, and the company has chosen to run
fewer trips rather than dilute them.

Two assistant guides advanced to guide during the season. The season ran at a
modest operating surplus, earlier than the plan assumed — which is one season,
not a trend.
