Polar Beacons Technologies Ltd. has been incorporated and has begun trading,
with two multi-year service agreements for environmental and infrastructure
monitoring.

The company employs six people, all resident in the territory. Two are on
structured training plans funded by the company.

It is not expected to be profitable before its third year, and the plan says so.
