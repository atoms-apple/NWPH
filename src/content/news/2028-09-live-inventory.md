Polar Express Stores has begun publishing live stock levels for both its
locations, to the web and to the Polar Express app.

Customers can see what is on the shelf right now, when that count last changed,
and reserve an item for collection.

The reason is specific to the North. In a community where the store is a walk or
a ride away in weather that can turn, and where an out-of-stock item may mean
waiting for the next flight, "is it actually there?" is the question that
matters most. No operator in the territory was answering it.

The feed updates from the point-of-sale system as items are sold and received.
It is not a promise — a count can be wrong, and the app says so where it is
uncertain — but it is a great deal better than driving over to find out.
