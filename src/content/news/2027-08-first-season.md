ArcTrek Expeditions has completed its first season of operations.

The company ran a single spring floe-edge programme with eleven seasonal staff.
The season was costed as a learning exercise rather than a profitable one, and
it was.

What the season established is that the model works operationally. Whether it
works commercially takes another two seasons to answer honestly.
