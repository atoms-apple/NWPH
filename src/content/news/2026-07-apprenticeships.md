Twenty-six apprentices have been placed for 2026, the largest intake since the
programme was consolidated across the portfolio in 2021.

Placements this year cover aircraft maintenance engineering at North Winds Air,
marine emergency duties and deck operations at Ima Sealift, construction trades
alongside two Illuvigaq builds, and retail management at Polar Express.

Schooling costs, travel and accommodation are covered in full. Apprentices are
employees from day one rather than students on placement.
