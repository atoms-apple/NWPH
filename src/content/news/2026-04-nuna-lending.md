Nuna Financial Services Ltd. has passed 200 active loans, seven years after it
began lending.

The company was structured deliberately as a lender rather than a deposit-taking
institution, and does not use the restricted term "bank".

Its underwriting model assesses seasonal and harvesting income as income, and
treats a thin credit file as a feature of the territory's credit infrastructure
rather than as applicant risk.
