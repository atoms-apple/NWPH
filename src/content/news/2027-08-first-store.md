Polar Express Stores has opened in Pangnirtung — the corporation's first
operating business, eleven months after incorporation.

Nine staff were recruited, none of whom had worked in retail before. Buying for
the first season was planned onto sealift eleven months before the doors opened,
which is the part of this business that cannot be improvised.

Retail was chosen ahead of tourism because a store trades every day of the year
rather than for eight weeks. A corporation with no revenue history needs that
more than it needs a good season.
