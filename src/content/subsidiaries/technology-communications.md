Technical work in Nunavut has largely been delivered remotely, or by contractors
flown in for a specific job. When a system fails between visits, the community
waits.

Polar Beacons is the corporation's second operating company, incorporated in
2028 and trading in its first year.

## What it does

Environmental and infrastructure monitoring, and field technical services, under
service agreements with operators working in the territory.

## Where it stands

First year of trading, with two service agreements and a team of six. The
company is not yet profitable and was not expected to be; the plan carries it to
break-even in its third year.

The binding constraint is not capital. It is finding and training technicians
who live here, which is also the entire point of the company.

## Employment

Technical staff are recruited locally and trained in post. Two of the six are on
structured training plans funded by the company.
