## What it does

Environmental and infrastructure monitoring, connectivity support, and field
technical services for operators working in the territory — with technicians
resident in Nunavut rather than flown in for each job.

## Operations

The company maintains monitoring equipment in remote locations and holds spares
in the territory, so a failure is a call-out rather than a wait for a part to
come north. Work is delivered under multi-year service agreements.

## Employment

Technical staff are recruited locally and trained in post, on the basis that the
labour market will not supply this workforce ready-made and waiting for it is
not a strategy.
