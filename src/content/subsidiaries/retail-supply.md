## What it does

General retail in Qikiqtani communities: groceries, hardware, harvesting and
outdoor equipment, and Inuit art and craft supplies.

## Operations

Buying is consolidated across stores and shipped on sealift, with air freight
reserved for perishables and urgent lines. Inventory is planned a season ahead,
which is the discipline the business lives or dies by.

## Employment

Store teams are recruited and promoted locally, with management training
delivered in community rather than requiring relocation south.
