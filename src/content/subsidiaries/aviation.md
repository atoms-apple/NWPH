## What it does

Charter and scheduled service in the Qikiqtani region: passengers, freight,
medical transfers and camp support.

## Operations

Aircraft are selected for gravel strips, short runways and cold-weather
operations. The company holds an Air Operator Certificate and runs the
operational control, maintenance and safety management systems that
certification requires.

## Employment

Flight crew and licensed maintenance engineers are scarce and mobile
everywhere. The company sponsors training for Nunavummiut entering both
streams, on the basis that recruiting from the south indefinitely is not a plan.
